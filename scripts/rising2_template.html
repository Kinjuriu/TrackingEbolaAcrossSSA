How this was made

A 3D spike map built in the browser with three.js. The ground is real: a cloud-free Sentinel-2 satellite mosaic, sharpened over eastern Congo, Kasaï and West Africa, shaded with relief from an open elevation model so the Albertine Rift and the Rwenzori stand out. Rivers, lakes and borders come from Natural Earth; current DRC provinces from geoBoundaries.

Each spike’s height is proportional to confirmed cases, on one scale for the whole film, which is why 2014 towers over everything and Kenya’s three cases barely leave the ground. 2026 spikes sit on one town per affected province. Totals come from reporting dates; between those dates the curve, and each province’s share of it, is interpolated.

Figures for the 2026 outbreak are provisional and are revised as data is reviewed. One linked case was also confirmed in France. Earlier outbreaks show their final totals.
